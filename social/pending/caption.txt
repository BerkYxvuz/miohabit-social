Missed a day? That's data, not a failure.

One skipped rep doesn't break a habit — it's the second miss in a row that starts rewiring the pattern. So the rule isn't "never miss" — it's never miss twice. If you skip today, show up tomorrow no matter how small: one push-up, one page, one minute of stretching. Log the tiny version in Miohabit anyway — a scaled-down win keeps the pattern alive, and the pattern is what actually compounds.
