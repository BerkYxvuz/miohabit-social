Stop trying to build habits from zero. Stack them instead.

Habit stacking means anchoring a new habit to one you already do on autopilot — brush teeth → floss one tooth, pour coffee → write one line in your journal, sit down at your desk → open Miohabit and check today's list. The existing routine becomes the trigger, so you skip the hardest part: remembering to start. Pick one habit you never miss, and bolt the new one right onto it.
