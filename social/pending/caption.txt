You don't have to get everything right today. Just take one tiny step — drink a glass of water, read for 5 minutes, do one rep. Habits aren't built by big decisions, they're built by small repeats.

Motivation comes and goes, sometimes it doesn't show up at all. But if you have a system, you can keep going even without it. Today's small step is a thank-you note to tomorrow's you.
