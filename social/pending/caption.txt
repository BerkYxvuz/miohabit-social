You don't build habits. You become someone who has them.

Chasing an outcome burns out fast, but every time you complete the small action, you're proving a small fact about who you are — a runner, a writer, a person who shows up. That's why the habit sticks once the identity shift happens first: the behavior stops feeling like a task and starts feeling like just... you. Next time you log a habit in Miohabit, don't think "I finished my to-do." Think "that's exactly the kind of person I am."
