Your willpower isn't the problem. Your surroundings are.

Most habits fail because they depend on motivation in the exact moment temptation is loudest. Flip the setup instead: put your running shoes by the door, keep the good snacks at eye level, and leave your phone charging in another room at night. When the cue for the habit you want is visible and the cue for the habit you don't is buried, the decision gets made before you even have to make it. Log it in Miohabit once the environment does the heavy lifting — you're just tracking the win.
